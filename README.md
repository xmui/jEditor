## Everything you need, nothing you don't

- **Open a folder and go.** Every photo shows up straight away. There's nothing to import and nothing uploaded, and your files stay where they are.
- **Quick fixes.** Rotate, crop and straighten. Fix the light and color with a few sliders or one-click Auto, or pick a look.
- **Fix a whole batch at once.** Rotate every selected photo in one go, crop them all like the last one, or copy one photo's adjustments onto as many as you like. Rename a whole folder in one step.
- **Sort as you go.** Flag the keepers, move them into folders, and delete with undo.
- **Undo anything.** Every step can be undone and redone, even a delete. Your originals stay safe until you save.

## Convert to save space

- **A whole folder at once,** to AVIF, JPEG XL, WebP, JPEG, PNG or TIFF. iPhone HEIC photos too, so any app can open them.
- **See before you commit.** The quality slider marks where a photo still looks the same, a close-up shows the original beside the result, and you see how much space you'll free.
- **Nothing lost.** Dates, camera details, flags and edits go with each photo. Keep both, or replace the originals and get them back with one undo.

## Great with scans and old photos

- **Scan straight into the folder,** and each scan shows up as it lands. (The Windows app.)
- **Bring old prints back.** Take the fade off with Restore color, find dust and scratches for you, and remove the dots from a magazine scan.
- **Dates in batches.** Give a scanned order one date, shift dates when a camera's clock was wrong, or number them in scan order.

- Looks: film stocks and creative looks you can dial in and save as your own
- Masks: linear, radial and brush, plus the sky and the subject found for you

- Perspective and lens fixes

**Old photos**
- Restore color for faded prints and slides
- Find dust, spot removal with heal or clone, and red-eye
- Scratches and creases: drag along one, or find them for you

**Paint**
- Layers with opacity and blend modes, masks, groups, clipping and adjustment layers

- A home page, a short tour and a guide (`F1`)

## Get it

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | [**Download the installer**](https://github.com/xmui/jEditor/releases/latest) `jEditor-Setup-x.y.z.exe`. No admin needed. Or the portable zip. |
| **Mac** | [**Download the .dmg**](https://github.com/xmui/jEditor/releases/latest). Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later. Or with Homebrew: `brew tap xmui/jeditor https://github.com/xmui/jEditor && brew install --cask jeditor` |
| **Linux** | [**AppImage**](https://github.com/xmui/jEditor/releases/latest) for any distribution (updates itself), `.deb` for Ubuntu, Debian and Mint, `.pacman` for Arch |
| **Web** | [**xmui.github.io/jEditor**](https://xmui.github.io/jEditor/) in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install. |

A lighter Mac build on Tauri (`jEditor-x.y.z-mac-tauri.dmg`) is in the [betas](https://github.com/xmui/jEditor/releases) for now.

<details>
<summary>"Windows protected your PC" or "jEditor can't be opened"?</summary>

jEditor isn't code-signed yet, so the first launch needs one extra click:

- **Windows:** click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to jEditor.

It only asks once. More in [Installing](docs/installing.md).
</details>

## Quick start

1. **Open a folder**, or drop one onto the window.
2. **Look and sort.** `S` shows one photo, `G` the grid, `←` `→` move between them. `P` flags a keeper, `[` `]` rotate, `C` crops.
3. **Fix one, then the rest.** `E` opens Adjust, and `Ctrl+Shift+C` copies what you did. In the grid, select the others and `Ctrl+Shift+V` pastes it onto them all.
4. **Convert** from the home page or the ⋯ menu.

`Ctrl+K` (`⌘K` on a Mac) finds any command, photo or folder. Press `F1` in the app for the guide, or read [Using jEditor](docs/using-jeditor.md).

## Platforms

The Windows, Mac and Linux apps have everything and keep up with each other. The web version is lighter: it has no sidebar of your drives and no extra windows, and scanning is in the Windows app only. The full list is in [Platforms](docs/platforms.md).

## Build and test

```sh
npm ci && npm test         # the tests, in headless Chrome
npm start                  # the web version at http://localhost:3000
cd mac && npm ci && cd .. && npm run mac   # the Mac and Linux app
```

Windows builds with `./scripts/build.ps1`. [Developing](docs/developing.md) has the rest: building each app, releases and betas. `node scripts/readme-shots.js` takes this page's screenshots again.
