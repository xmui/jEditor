# 2.0 polish brief (from the owner, 2026-11-18)

For the Fable design chat's UI / polish pass before 2.0.

- **Scope:** a look over everything. Start with dialogs, menus and Settings: they look inconsistent and plain/cheap.
- **Direction:** jEditor's own look, a mix of Lightroom and Apple: Adjust and Paint panels dense and pro (Lightroom); Photos, menus, dialogs and chrome calm (Apple).
- **Main references: Photomator and Pixelmator Pro.** In effect they're Apple's Lightroom and Photoshop, and the closest match for the mix above. Study how they lay out panels, tools, sliders, menus and dialogs.
- **Work like an outside design firm:** treat this as a company hiring a design firm to review and rework the product before a major release. Be candid, not polite to the existing work. Deliver what a firm would:
  1. **An audit:** a critical review of every screen, dialog, menu and flow against Photomator, Pixelmator Pro and general design standards. Show what's wrong and why, with screenshots, and rank each issue by how much it matters.
  2. **A direction:** a short design-language proposal (type, spacing, colour, depth, icons, motion, components), with mockups of key screens for the owner to sign off.
  3. **The rework:** small PRs that apply the signed-off direction, starting with the shared components (buttons, dialogs, menus, Settings), then each mode.
- **Be critical:** review jEditor as a designer would in general, not just against this list. Call out anything that looks off, cluttered, dated or unclear, with reasons.
- **Appearance:** follow the system (light or dark), both polished equally.
- **Density:** medium, between Apple and Lightroom.
- **Finished dialogs and menus:** depth and soft shadows; one button style (same primary/secondary, sizes, placement everywhere); better type (clear hierarchy, tighter spacing, on a grid).
- **Settings:** Mac-style sidebar: sections in a left list, one page each, grouped rows.
- **Icons:** one clean, consistent line-icon set across the app.
- **Type:** the system font (SF Pro on Mac, Segoe UI Variable on Windows).
- **Accent:** keep jEditor's accent, used more sparingly.
- **Motion:** subtle: short, quick fades and slides; nothing you wait on.
- **Nothing is sacred:** changes may be proposed anywhere.
- **How:** fix small things directly; mockups for bigger changes, owner picks.
- **Timing:** owner still planning; don't start until the owner says.
